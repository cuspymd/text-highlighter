Getting Started with Marks

Marks lets you highlight text on any web page and finds it again the next time you visit. Here is everything it can do.

Highlight text

Select text on a page and a highlighter icon appears next to it. Click the icon to open the color palette, then pick a color.

Change the color or remove it

Click an existing highlight to bring up its controls. From there you can switch its color or delete it.

Use the right-click menu

Select text and right-click to highlight straight from the context menu, without using the icon.

Keyboard shortcuts

Five shortcut slots highlight the selected text right away. Slots 1 to 4 come with Ctrl+Shift+1 through 4 (Command+Shift on macOS); slot 5 has no key until you assign one. Which color each slot uses is set in Settings.

Minimap

Marks along the right edge of the page show where your highlights are. Click one to jump to it. You can turn the minimap off in Settings.

Find your highlights again

The toolbar icon lists the highlights on the current page - click one to scroll to it. The highlighted pages list collects every page you have highlighted, and lets you export or import them as a backup.
